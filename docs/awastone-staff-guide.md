# WAVR — Staff Guide (Awastone)

**What it is:** WAVR is our digital waiver system. Guests sign on a phone instead of paper, and every signed waiver lands straight in our dashboard.

**Why we use it:**

- **It's our legal record.** If anything ever goes wrong, the signed waiver — with the guest's signature, the date and time of their trip, and what they told us about their health — is the proof we rely on. Insurers and lawyers ask for these.
- **We see medical info before the trip, not after.** Anyone who declares a condition is flagged in the dashboard so guides know before they're on the water.
- **No more lost or soggy paperwork.** Nothing to file, nothing to lose, and we can find any guest in seconds.
- **It works without signal.** Important at the river.

---

## 1. Getting guests to sign

Each trip type has its own waiver link and QR code:

- **GC Waiver** — awastone.wavr.app/sign/gc-waiver
- **Boulders Waiver** — awastone.wavr.app/sign/boulders-waiver
- **PG Raft/Kayak Waiver** — awastone.wavr.app/sign/pg-raft-kayak-waiver
- **Multisport Course Waiver** — awastone.wavr.app/sign/multisport-course-waiver

**Use the right one.** The waiver a guest signs is tied to the trip they're doing. Signing the wrong one means our record doesn't match what they actually did.

Guests can reach it by:

- **Scanning the QR code** (printed at base / on the vehicle) with their phone camera
- **Tapping a link** we've sent with their booking confirmation
- **Using a base device** if they don't have a phone on them

They don't need to download anything or create an account.

## 2. What the guest fills in

The waiver asks one question per screen, so it's quick on a phone:

1. **Trip date and departure time** — they pick the day and the session.
2. **Their details** — name, age, email, where they're from.
3. **Medical question** — if they answer yes, they're asked to describe it.
4. **The waiver text** — they scroll through it and tick to agree.
5. **Signature** — they sign with a finger, and they're done.

### Groups and families

After someone signs, there's an **"ADD ANOTHER PERSON"** button. Use it for the rest of the group — it keeps the same trip date and time, so you don't re-enter it each time. Tap **"WE'RE ALL DONE"** when the group is finished.

### Under 18s

If a guest enters an age under 18, the waiver **requires a parent or guardian**:

- The guardian's **full name** must be entered
- The **guardian** signs, not the child

In the dashboard that guest shows **"Signed by guardian · [name]"** under their name. If an under-18 has signed for themselves, that's not valid — get the guardian to redo it.

### Two things that only appear on some waivers

- **Free photos (GC Waiver only):** on the email screen there's a tick box to have trip photos sent to that email. Only on the GC Waiver.
- **"Have you been with us before?" (Multisport Course only):** returning guests can confirm nothing medical has changed and go straight to signing. Only on the Multisport Course Waiver.

## 3. No signal at the river

WAVR keeps working with no reception.

- An **orange bar** appears at the top: *"No signal — waivers will sync automatically when you reconnect."*
- Guests sign exactly as normal.
- Waivers are saved **on that device** and upload when it's back online.

**Important — this is the one thing that can lose a waiver:**

- Waivers signed offline sit on **that one device** until it reconnects.
- **When you're back in signal or on wifi, open the waiver page again on that same device.** That's what triggers the upload.
- **Don't** clear the browser, delete history, or use private/incognito mode on a device holding unsynced waivers.
- **Check the dashboard afterwards** to confirm the day's guests are actually there.

If something doesn't appear, say so the same day — the sooner we know, the better the chance of recovering it.

## 4. Checking the dashboard

Log in at **awastone.wavr.app** and go to **Guests**.

### Confirming a trip's numbers

Guests are grouped by trip, with a headcount on each:

- *Sat 10 Oct · 10am — 5 guests*
- *Sat 10 Oct · 2pm — 2 guests*

Before a trip, check the headcount matches who's actually turning up. If it's short, someone has probably signed the wrong date or time.

### Finding people fast

- **Today / Tomorrow** buttons, or pick any date
- Filter by **departure time**, by **waiver**, by **Medical: yes/no**, by **Under 18 / 18+**, and by who wants **photos**
- Or search by name

**Before every trip, check `Today` + `Medical: yes`.** That's the list of people whose health notes the guide needs to know about.

### Looking at one guest

Tap anyone to open their record — medical notes, guardian details, email, age, country, and their signature.

### Fixing a wrong date or time

If someone signed for the wrong session:

1. Tap the guest to expand their record
2. On the trip line, tap **Change**
3. Set the correct date and departure time
4. Tap **Save trip**

Do this rather than asking them to sign again — it keeps one clean record.

### Getting a list out

**Export CSV** downloads whatever is currently on screen, with your filters applied. Handy for a trip manifest, or for pulling the email list of guests wanting photos.

## 5. If something goes wrong

- **Guest can't open the link** — check their signal, or let them sign on a base device.
- **Page is slow the first time** — it wakes up after a quiet period; it's quick after that.
- **Guest signed the wrong waiver** — have them sign the correct one; tell the office so the wrong record can be removed.
- **Guest signed the wrong day/time** — fix it with **Change** (above). No need to re-sign.
- **Someone's missing from the dashboard** — see the offline section. Most likely their waiver is still sitting unsynced on a device.
- **An under-18 signed for themselves** — not valid. Get a parent or guardian to complete it properly.

---

## 6. Looking after guest information

These waivers contain personal and medical information about our guests. Under the Privacy Act we're responsible for looking after it.

- **Don't** share the dashboard login. Everyone who needs access gets their own.
- **Don't** screenshot guest details or send them over personal messaging.
- **Don't** leave the dashboard open on an unattended device at base.
- **Do** lock any shared tablet or phone when you put it down.
- Only look up guests when you need to for the job.
